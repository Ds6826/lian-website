Why would an observability or memory vendor not simply build this?

Immutable and non-sampled retention

Evidence must survive sampling, changing state, and ordinary operational retention windows.

Decision-boundary reconstruction

The product must preserve what was available and cited at the legally significant moment, not merely retrieve what is known now.

Attestation workflow

Compliance, model-risk, and legal reviewers need a portable record with explicit integrity and custody boundaries.

Supporting engineering evidence
