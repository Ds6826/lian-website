Can you reconstruct one consequential AI decision?

That is the narrow question behind RIAD-1. The benchmark does not ask whether a memory system retrieves a pleasant conversation. It asks whether an evidence layer can reopen the boundary around a disputed decision after the underlying facts have changed.

2026-07-25 · Lians · 6 min read · Open benchmark

The fixture

One underwriting decision, two known facts, and one fact learned later.

RIAD-1 models a credit-underwriting agent. Before approval, the system records verified income and debt-to-income evidence. After approval, it records a missed payment. The benchmark also accepts an authenticated OpenTelemetry GenAI span and writes a decision linked to its model, policy, session, input and output hashes, and cited evidence IDs.

The central correctness test is simple: the exported decision-time snapshot must contain the two facts available before approval and must not leak the later missed payment backward into history.

Six checks

A pass must produce evidence, not a marketing assertion.

1 · Exact point-in-time reconstruction

Prior evidence appears, the later fact does not, and cited evidence IDs exactly match the decision record.

2 · Required provenance coverage

Agent, session, model, model version, policy version, decision time, knowledge cutoff, evidence IDs, and input/output hashes are populated.

3 · Evidence-pack integrity

Recomputing the deterministic SHA-256 value matches the hash shipped with the exported pack.

4 · GenAI OTLP ingestion

The authenticated OTLP/HTTP receiver accepts and retains the OpenTelemetry GenAI span used by the fixture.

5 · Audit-payload tamper detection

The benchmark deliberately changes a v2 audit payload and requires chain verification to return tampered.

6 · Local replay latency guard

Evidence-pack P95 must remain below three seconds in the local functional environment.

What this does not prove

The limitations belong beside the score.

Not a production load test

The fixture runs offline against ephemeral SQLite. Production Postgres throughput, concurrency, and durability require a separate benchmark.

Not certified WORM or legal attestation

The test proves application-level tamper evidence. Storage certification, retention controls, anchoring, and legal attestation are deployment concerns.

Not automatic trace correlation

The fixture explicitly stores trace and span IDs in decision metadata. Automatic OTLP trace-to-decision linking is not claimed.

Not universal model-output replay

Exact output reproduction also depends on preserving provider behavior, model determinism, tool state, and artifacts not submitted to Lians.

Versioned integrity boundary

New v2 audit rows hash canonical JSON payloads. Historical v1 rows remain verifiable but did not include the JSON payload in their hash.
