# Frontend freeze

The public marketing frontend is frozen from July 15 through July 22, 2026.

During the freeze, frontend changes are limited to verified bugs affecting correctness, accessibility, security, analytics, application submission, or notifications. Copy, layout, positioning, cohort structure, and visual changes require evidence from real prospect behavior. Scheduling is handled manually by email unless prospect behavior justifies adding booking software.

The public cohort remains seven companies until a real partner joins. Update the count only after that event is confirmed, and update every public occurrence in the same bug-fix change.

Before merging a permitted fix, verify:

- The primary CTA name is “Join founding cohort” or “Apply to the founding cohort” and targets `/design-partners` or its `#apply` form.
- The API-key action remains available as a secondary developer path.
- No three-slot language has returned.
- Partner submission, notification, attribution, and confirmation behavior still pass.
